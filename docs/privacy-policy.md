# Privacy Policy for JAHC Manager

**Last updated:** July 9, 2026

## Overview
JAHC Manager ("the App") is a club management application for HEMA (Historical European Martial Arts) clubs.

## Data Collection
The App collects the following information:
- Account credentials (username/password) for authentication
- Club member profiles and training data
- Tournament participation records

## Data Usage
All collected data is used solely to provide club management functionality. We do not sell or share personal data with third parties.

## Data Storage
Data is stored securely on our servers and is accessible only to authorized club members.
